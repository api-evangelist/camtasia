A video SOP is a standard operating procedure delivered in video form. Videos can improve SOPs by adding context that text alone may not show, especially for software tasks, onboarding, quality checks, and other step-heavy procedures. Start with a process that employees often misunderstand, or that takes too long to explain in writing.
