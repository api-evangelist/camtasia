Your training request list keeps growing, but your production team doesn’t. Another department wants a walkthrough by Friday, a policy changed last week, and the video you finished last month already needs an update. Scalable employee training supports more learners, roles, and locations without requiring proportional increases in production time, headcount, or cost.
