An explainer video is a short, goal-driven video that uses visuals and narration to clearly explain a product, service, process, or idea. Explainer videos help people quickly understand a product, service, process, or idea. Marketing teams, product teams, training teams, and educators use them to show what matters and help viewers take the next step.
