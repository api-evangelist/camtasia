OBS Studio is built for live streaming and screen recording, not video editing. It doesn’t include tools for trimming, adding effects, or polishing your footage. To turn a recording into a professional video, you’ll need a separate editor like Camtasia Editor, DaVinci Resolve, Shotcut, or Adobe Premiere Pro.
