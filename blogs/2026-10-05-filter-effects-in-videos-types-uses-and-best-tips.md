Filter effects aren’t just for social media and selfies. Filter effects in videos are preset digital adjustments that change footage by adjusting color, brightness, contrast, sharpness, or style. Used intentionally, they’re a practical way to improve video quality, correct lighting and color, and shape how your audience experiences your content.
