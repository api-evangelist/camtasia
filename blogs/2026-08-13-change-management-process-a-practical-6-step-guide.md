Change management is a structured process for preparing, equipping, and supporting people as an organization adopts new processes, technologies, or structures. It combines planning, communication, training, implementation, reinforcement, and review so teams understand the desired outcome and can sustain the change over time. Key Takeaways Document any internal process as you do it.
