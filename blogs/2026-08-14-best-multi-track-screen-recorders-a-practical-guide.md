Multi-track screen recording captures multiple audio and video sources simultaneously and keeps them on separate tracks. You can then adjust each source independently, such as system audio, microphone input, webcam footage, screen video, or cursor details. This makes it easier to refine tutorials, presentations, and demonstrations without re-recording the full video.
