Knowledge-Centered Support (KCS), also known as Knowledge-Centered Service, is a customer service methodology designed to manage and share knowledge more effectively. It focuses on capturing, organizing, and reusing knowledge to solve customer problems quickly and efficiently. Customers expect quick, accurate answers.
