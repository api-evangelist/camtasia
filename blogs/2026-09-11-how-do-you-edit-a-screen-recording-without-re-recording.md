Your math walkthrough is ready for review when you spot one wrong menu selection halfway through the recording. There’s no reason to re-record the entire presentation just because of one mistake. Editing a screen recording means trimming, splitting, cutting, tightening audio, adding captions, and exporting to turn a raw capture into a clear final video.
