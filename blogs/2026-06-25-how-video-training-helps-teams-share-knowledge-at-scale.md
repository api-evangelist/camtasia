Valuable organizational knowledge often remains trapped in the minds of subject matter experts (SMEs). Without a knowledge-sharing culture, critical details can be lost in long meetings, in chat threads, or in outdated wiki pages. Video training is recorded instruction that shows a process, workflow, or concept in action.
