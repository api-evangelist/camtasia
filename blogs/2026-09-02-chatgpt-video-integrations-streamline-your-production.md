When creating an instructional video, many trainers and educators quickly realize that video production involves far more than just editing footage. Scripts, captions, summaries, social posts, translations, and content planning can quickly turn a simple project into hours of extra work. That’s where AI tools like OpenAI’s ChatGPT fit in.
