Choosing between a browser screen recorder and a desktop screen recorder feels straightforward until you’re three weeks into a new onboarding program and realize your LMS won’t accept the files your tool exports by default. That’s the situation many L&D teams hit after they’ve already standardized on a recording workflow. The browser tool was fast.
